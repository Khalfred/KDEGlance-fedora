# Screenshot provenance

The five PNGs were captured from the real 1.0.0 Qt widgets. The design is unchanged
in 1.0.1; these original captures are retained. They were made by
`tests/render_screenshots.py` using Qt's offscreen platform. The controls and
placeholder are the application's own painting code; video comes from the real
QMediaPlayer/QVideoSink. Controls are held visible for capture. These are not
interface mockups and do not show a native desktop/compositor session.

- image-preview.png: original geometric image, controls hidden.
- image-controls.png: the same image after a real wheel zoom event, controls shown.
- audio-cover.png: actual MP3 embedded art, paused playback and controls.
- audio-placeholder.png: WAV without embedded artwork, actual glass placeholder.
- video-preview.png: decoded procedural FFmpeg test clip and real controls.

All underlying media are generated by tests/generate_fixtures.py; no third-party
photos, recordings, movie frames or design-sheet bitmaps are included. The
screenshots and generated media are GPL-3.0-or-later with KDEGlance.

## Fedora port screenshots (port-*.png)

Added by the unofficial Fedora port in October 2026. Unlike the captures above,
these are native windows: the installed 1.0.1 Fedora package (release 10) running
in a Plasma 6 Wayland session on Ultramarine 44 (Fedora 44 base), with real KWin
placement. Each was captured with `spectacle -b -n -a` (active window) and is
unedited. The icons come from the capturing desktop's icon theme (WhiteSur).

- port-pdf.png: a 6-page PDF generated from this repository's README.md with
  QTextDocument/QPdfWriter, showing the page sidebar and page counter.
- port-code.png: kdeglance/fileinfo.py from this repository, highlighted by
  KSyntaxHighlighting (Breeze Dark).
- port-executable.png: the information panel for /usr/bin/dolphin.
- port-folder.png: the information panel for /usr/share/wallpapers.
- port-image.png: a still from FFmpeg's procedural `mandelbrot` source
  (1600x1000), in a window fitted to the image.
- port-video.png: a 1080x1920 clip from the same `mandelbrot` source, in the
  enlarged window used for vertical video.
- port-social-preview.jpg: 1280x640 collage of the above for GitHub's social
  preview, composed with QPainter.

No personal files, photos or recordings are included. The screenshots are
GPL-3.0-or-later with KDEGlance.
